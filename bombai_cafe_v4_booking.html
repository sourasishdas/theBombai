Our Story

A Taste of
Real Bombai

We bring you the vibrant streets of Mumbai — the chai tapris, the Irani cafés, the coastal seafood stalls — reimagined in a space where every corner tells a story of the city we love.

From Galouti Kebabs that melt on your tongue to Malvani fish curries that take you straight to the Konkan coast, Bombai is not just a restaurant — it's an experience.
